# Story / Task block

Paste into the issue body and fill it in. A Task adds the procedure link under
References, or states that no procedure exists.

```markdown
## At a glance

<3-5 plain-English sentences: what this is and why it matters.>

## Context

**Why now:** <the trigger>
**What is missing:** <the observable gap>
**Value:** <what you gain, or what it costs not to do it>

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] <observable condition>
- [ ] <observable condition>

## Scope

**In:** <what this issue covers>
**Out:** <what it explicitly does not>

## References

- [<name>](<permalink>) — <what the reader gets from it>
- #<NN> — <how it relates>
```
