# Spike block

Paste into the issue body and fill it in. A Spike is bounded by time, and its
criterion is that something is written down.

```markdown
## At a glance

<3-5 plain-English sentences.>

## Context

**Why now:** <the trigger>
**What you do not know:** <the gap in knowledge>
**Value:** <what the answer unblocks>

## Question

<The question this spike answers, as a question.>

## Time-box

<days or sessions>

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] <The artifact> is written to <location>, recording <the decision or finding>.

## References

- [<prior art or source to read>](<URL>) — <why it is worth reading>
- #<NN> — <how it relates>
```
